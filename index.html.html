How the AI learns (reinforcement learning)

The AI is a Q-learning agent. Every frame it reads the situation — how far the ball is, and where the ball will cross its line (the intercept point) compared with its racket — and picks one of three moves: up, stay, or down.

It keeps a table Q[state][action] scoring how good each move is. After every frame it nudges those values: Q ← Q + a(r + g·maxQ' − Q). Rewards: +2 for returning the ball, −1 for missing, +2 for winning a point, plus a small dense reward for lining its racket up with the ball's intercept.

It begins by exploring (e = 1.0, mostly random) and gradually switches to exploiting what it has learned (e falls to 0.05). Watch the green line climb as it stops missing. Its memory is saved in this browser, so it keeps improving every time you return.
